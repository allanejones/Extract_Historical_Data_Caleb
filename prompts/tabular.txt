As a hydrogeologist, I am digitizing historical groundwater levels from handwritten well records throughout Illinois. The data are arranged in a table where columns are months, and rows represent the day of the month. The combination of the column and row create the date for the data record. For example, the record in the January column along the first row was observed on January 1. As such, data will never exist for dates that do not exist; for example, February 30. Data is not recorded in every cell of the table; there will be blank entries. Please return blank entries as "-". I need you to extract all the textual and numeric data into CSV format. Represent numerical values as standard US decimal numbers with no thousands separators. Represent blank, empty, or NA values as "-".
